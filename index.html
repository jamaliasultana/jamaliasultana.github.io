WACV 2027Egocentric vision · Human–object interaction

EgoWaste: A Hierarchical Egocentric Waste Dataset for Material Recovery

Jamalia Sultana, Ruwen Qin, Zhaozheng Yin

An egocentric benchmark for material recovery, with 35,754 annotated frames and 39,083 active-object instances across 15 materials and 56 fine-grained classes. Its annotation pipeline combines expert speech, foundation-model proposals, tracking, hand–object interaction, and human verification.

ACCV 2024Multimodal learning · Graph neural networks

Seeing Through Expert’s Eyes: Leveraging Radiologist Eye Gaze and Speech Report with Graph Neural Networks for Chest X-ray Image Classification

Jamalia Sultana, Ruwen Qin, Zhaozheng Yin

Uses radiologists’ gaze and transcribed reports to guide graph neural network learning for chest X-ray classification. Expert signals supervise training; inference uses the image alone.

Develops semi-supervised segmentation for patient-specific 3D reconstruction of the proximal femur from quantitative CT. The method addresses reconstruction with limited image annotations.
